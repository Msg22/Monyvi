## Owner manual execution plan

Use the existing owner-configured app/data without resetting financial data. Record environment/build, language/theme, and whether Voice availability is healthy before each Voice observation.

1. **Manual baseline:** Open Add Transaction from the FAB. Confirm Manual selected, ordinary required Amount, Account/Category normal-width row where space permits, optional-details row, no calculator before focus, and no Voice quota/error/loading notice.
2. **Amount focus:** Focus Amount. Confirm the native Android keyboard does not appear and the custom calculator does. Enter a grouped value/expression, use equals as needed, press Done, and confirm the calculator dismisses without navigation or persistence.
3. **Save:** Press header Save and confirm exactly one local transaction is created. Repeat validation with a missing required field and with recurring details expanded/collapsed.
4. **Draft switching:** Enter a draft, open calculator, switch Manual -> Voice -> Manual in an unlocked state. Confirm draft remains and calculator returns closed.
5. **Transfer:** Verify source and target amount focus remain independent. For a cross-currency transfer, edit target amount explicitly, Save, then verify the persisted/displayed converted amount and effective exchange relationship are the edited values rather than a stale quote.
6. **Manual while Voice blocked:** For any observable loading, consent-required, unavailable, burst-limited or daily-limit state, switch to Manual and confirm no Voice notice appears and local Manual Save remains usable.
7. **Voice locale states:** Compare EN idle, AR idle, EN daily-limit and AR daily-limit against the five approved references when those states are available. Verify unavailable is distinct from daily exhaustion and loading never invents a zero/limit.
8. **Recorder/permission:** On a real device, verify custom permission explanation -> native permission only after Continue; denial/revocation/Settings recovery; start/stop/pause/resume/discard; mode lock during recording/paused/finalizing/analyzing; back/cancel/background/restart cleanup; successful recording reaches the existing review flow.
9. **Responsive/theme/accessibility:** Validate ordinary/compact phone and any available wider/landscape context, light/dark, EN/AR RTL, font scales 1.35/2, safe-area clearance, focus order, hidden inactive tree, accessible names/roles/states and recovery focus. Record visual and accessibility evidence separately from functional results.
10. **Server/provider-only scenarios:** Preserve the existing quickstart server matrix for daily/burst, concurrency, provider-start accounting, timezone/reset, replay and cleanup. Do not treat the conditional Voice Maestro YAML as proof of these scenarios.
