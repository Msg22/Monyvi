### B1 — BLOCKER (grounding): a model transaction alias can borrow another SMS's sender

Add two-message cross-alias negative test. Also separately check sender alias provenance: lines 27–28 accept `alias === accountEvidence.name` without a genuine sender field/span; don't silently promote account names into `senderNames`.
