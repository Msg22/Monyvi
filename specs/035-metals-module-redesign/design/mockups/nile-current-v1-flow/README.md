## Selected direction

The set applies **Nile Current — Personal Reserve** as a warm, calm consumer-finance environment:

- Monyvi shell, Nile/slate palette, Inter-style UI typography, Lucide-style icons, 8 px spacing rhythm, and 44 px minimum controls.
- Nile green carries action, focus, connection, and constructive state. Gold and Silver identify metal only.
- Flat-at-rest surfaces, restrained paper-like atmosphere, open editorial hierarchy, and limited borders/shadows.
- No glass, decorative gradients, crypto cues, luxury-bullion advertising, ERP/register density, generic card-stack dashboard, or universal rules.
- Full-device proofs reserve top and bottom safe areas. Stack routes use a PageHeader-style back action and no invented bottom tabs.

Home and My Metals roots use the exact visible voice-first order: **Home, Accounts, microphone, Transactions, Metals**, with the correct selected tab. Live Rates, Holding Detail, forms, focused Delete/Restore confirmations, History, Undo, and recovery are stack routes without bottom tabs.

## Consolidation and reusable patterns

- Sold and Disposed details remain separate because financial and non-financial meaning must be visible.
- Undo consequence is shown for a sale because it has richer linked account/result reversal. Reuse its shell for disposal with non-sale rows and no account/result reversal.
- Dirty exit adds no redundant PNG. Reuse the focused confirmation shell from 14/18 with exact actions **Keep editing** and **Discard changes**; returning to the same form preserves inputs and live preview.
- Pending submission reuses the current safe-area action region: disable duplicate actions, retain facts, and show one in-progress state.
- Failed local completion preserves visible facts and input with plain recovery copy and one explicit retry. It must not present success.
- Loading/empty/error/offline proofs remain deferred; no normal-flow screen claims them.
- **Automatic reconciliation recovery (V1 requirement, no separate PNG):** While reconciliation is incomplete, show `Checking changes` with `This holding changed on another device. We’re checking the holding and account before showing the final result.`, keep the last complete state effective, lock financial actions, and offer `Try sync again`. After the first complete valid server-accepted action becomes canonical, show `Holding changed on another device` with `Another change was saved first. We kept it and safely removed this device’s pending change.` and `View holding`. Rejected evidence stays internal audit-only and never appears in normal History.
